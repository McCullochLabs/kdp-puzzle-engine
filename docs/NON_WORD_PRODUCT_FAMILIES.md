# Non-Word Product Families

Recommended family directions are planning concepts subject to strict evidence:

- Nostalgia Through the Decades: Word Search, themed word games/trivia, Mixed, then rights-safe Cryptograms.
- Senior Large Print: Sudoku, Cryptograms, Mixed, and word games after typography and spacing checks.
- Road Trip Classics: Word Search, Scramble + Trivia, Mixed, and selected rights-safe Cryptograms.
- Hobby Brain Games: gardening, camping, cooking, classic cars, birding, fishing, quilting, and hiking only in suitable formats.
- Signature Sudoku: classic, progressive, large-print, letters, shapes, and mixed-symbol variants based on actual solving experience rather than cosmetic themes.

A cross-format extension is not a duplicate merely because its topic matches an existing Word Search. Same-format title/content overlap remains a duplication risk. A bundle becomes meaningful when at least two or three distinct formats share one honest topic identity and have customer-safe files.
