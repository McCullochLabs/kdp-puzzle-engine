# Non-Word Content Expansion

All formats use the shared topic taxonomy, but each applies its own eligibility rules.

- Scramble vocabulary: topic fit, format fit, duplicate protection, provenance, editorial approval.
- Trivia: question, answer, topic fit, independent source, factual verification status, source revision, ambiguity/outdated review, editorial approval.
- Cryptogram: prompt, topic fit, original/public-domain/licensed/rights-cleared status, provenance, revision, editorial approval.
- Sudoku: no content provider is required; generation must preserve the unique-solution contract.

Generated candidates remain unreviewed. Gemini may propose trivia, original sayings, or vocabulary only after explicit confirmation, and Gemini is never authoritative fact evidence. Campaigns must measure an exact format-specific shortage and inspect local candidates first.

Legacy strings may be counted as source material for audit purposes, but they are not silently promoted to reviewed capacity.
