# Product QA

Product QA is a local prepress inspection that runs after the interior and cover exist and before final approval. It complements the existing production proof and marketplace preflight; it does not replace them.

## What it checks

- Required package files and provenance records
- PDF readability, page dimensions, unexpected blank pages, and repeated substantial page text
- Presence of a solution or answer-key section
- Representative first, middle, and last puzzle/solution pages plus section dividers
- Puzzle-type-specific text-size review thresholds
- Text entering the critical trim-edge zone
- Front-cover print dimensions
- Existing visual-quality rules for topic fit, audience context, title hierarchy, series direction, puzzle-type correctness, and verified cover facts
- Unusually frequent artwork reuse, with intentional series consistency treated separately

## Results

- **QA Blocked** — a factual production defect must be corrected.
- **QA Needs Review** — no blocker was found, but a visual or editorial judgment remains.
- **QA Passed Locally** — the automated local checks found no blocker or warning.

These statuses never mean “KDP approved” or guarantee acceptance by any marketplace. Final review remains a human checkpoint.

Product QA runs in the background from Publishing Center so a large interior does not freeze the interface. Reports are written only when the operator or active generation route requests them.
