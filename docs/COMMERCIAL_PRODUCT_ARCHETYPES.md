# Commercial Product Archetypes

Archetypes are versioned planning profiles stored in `publishing/commercial_profiles.py`. They shape recommendations and listing emphasis; they are not templates that automatically create products.

Implemented archetypes:

- Nostalgia
- Senior Large Print
- Hobby Enthusiast
- Travel
- Seasonal
- History
- Giftable
- Relaxation
- Family / Group Activity
- Educational
- Challenge / Expert
- Device-Friendly Digital
- Faith / Inspiration

Each profile records preferred puzzle types, audience, visual expectations, content requirements, marketplace fit, listing emphasis, merchandising emphasis, risks, and format requirements.

## Important rules

- Word Search needs theme specificity, audience fit, a use case, topic-grounded vocabulary, and meaningful mini-theme breadth.
- Sudoku primarily sells the solving experience: quantity, difficulty, readability, progression, and variant. A cosmetic theme alone is weak differentiation.
- Cryptograms need a clear content identity and rights-safe original, public-domain, or licensed messages.
- Scramble + Trivia should feel like a themed social, occasion, or memory experience; trivia requires verification.
- Mixed Brain Games needs balanced variety under one coherent concept, with meaningful section transitions.
- Senior and large-print positioning must be substantiated by typography, spacing, contrast, hierarchy, and navigation—not merely by subtitle wording.
