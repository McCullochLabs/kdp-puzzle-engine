# Catalog Intelligence

Catalog Intelligence reads the existing Publishing Center database and explains what the local catalog already contains, where products overlap, and which locally supported ideas are closest to production.

It provides three operator views:

- **Fastest to Production** — strong local readiness with transparent deductions for overlap.
- **Catalog Gaps** — formats or audiences that are missing within established topics.
- **Series Ideas** — locally supported extensions of recognizable series.

Scores are planning priorities, not sales forecasts. The engine does not invent search volume, revenue, competition, demand, or marketplace acceptance. Every recommendation includes local reasons and overlap evidence.

The overlap detector compares normalized title, topic, puzzle format, audience, and series signals. Its candidate index prevents a full all-against-all comparison as the catalog grows.

Catalog Intelligence never changes a theme, creates a book, updates a marketplace status, or publishes a listing.
