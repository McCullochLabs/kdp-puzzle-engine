# Organization of existing ungrouped vocabulary

Organized 520 existing words into 537 proposed group/topic links. Evaluated 36 focused niche lists; 36 contain ungrouped candidates. Retained 630 root-suggestion links separately for triage only. No new words were invented.

Every association remains pending editorial review. This additive review batch does not change the master bank, classifier, taxonomy, approval decisions, or production inputs. Existing schemas are used. Niche labels sit beneath existing resolved parent topics; introducing independent taxonomy topics requires a later approved dataset update.

These groups can supply vocabulary to Word Search and Scramble/Mixed Brain Games. They are not verified trivia or authored cryptogram passages. No Sudoku or book generation occurred.

Already-grouped words are listed as context and excluded from new candidates. Catalog unassigned labels were checked against the live master groups, editorial overlay, and trusted/approved saved store. Root suggestions have false associations such as ARTERIAL under Arts Creativity and Making. They remain unassigned and are excluded from all organized candidate records.

Ambiguous terms and specialist vocabulary still need sense, spelling, audience, and topic-fit review. Safety filters preserve existing exact-term gates; passing them is not trademark clearance. Dictionary presence is not proof of suitability. Mushroom terms are vocabulary only, not identification or edibility guidance.
