# Definition-backed organization of ungrouped vocabulary

Organized **29,620 additional existing words** into **620 groups**. Of these, 7,773 words have focused semantic niche anchors and 21,847 are organized on named general vocabulary shelves. Earlier batches and existing topic assignments were excluded.

Each selected word has a WordNet definition and sense ID. Group anchors use semantic meaning relationships, not spelling substrings. Named entities, existing safety flags, rejected topic links, derogatory or explicit definitions, obsolete senses, and low-evidence rare general words remain held. Alternate meanings remain visible and unresolved.

All associations are **pending editorial review**. No approvals, master bank, saved store, taxonomy, UI, publishing, production pipeline, books, or covers were changed. These are additive research datasets. General vocabulary shelves are not specialist niche claims or full-book capacity certificates.

WordNet 3.0 is a historical lexical resource; definitions can contain dated phrasing and do not establish current scientific nomenclature. Specialist words and alternate meanings need contextual editorial review. Regular inflections use the documented WordNet Morphy rules and remain explicitly marked for morphology review. Competing base lemmas are held. Base forms and inflections are indexed together in lexeme_families.json to prevent artificial capacity inflation. The supplied license applies to all WordNet-derived definitions and relationships.

Vocabulary is suitable for review for Word Search and Scramble/Mixed Brain Games; definitions were not converted into approved trivia or cryptogram text. Sudoku has no corresponding word pool.

**320,366 words remain unassigned**, with a complete residual list and reason-specific queues. These include forms without dictionary definitions, low-familiarity terms, names, and unsuitable senses. Nothing was forced into a topic merely to reduce the count.

Sources: [Princeton WordNet](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/), [database format](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/documentation/wndb5wn), [license](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use). Download and source checksums appear in organization_audit.json.
