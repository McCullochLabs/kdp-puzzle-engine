# After Hours adult vocabulary research

Pending review only. No production banks, books, approvals, gates, UI, or generators changed. Standalone non-graphic words and object names only; no sexual act narratives.

The curated pool combines profanity/minced oaths, cheeky flirtation, lingerie/adult accessories, bar service, and alcoholic drinks. WordNet exact lemma evidence is included per candidate, with local offsets and definitions. These definitions are source evidence, not proposed puzzle copy. APA and Cambridge references independently attest the few explicitly cited terms. Unattested terms are held rather than invented or approved.

Excluded by policy: minors, hateful slurs, protected brands, graphic act descriptors, invented concatenations, arbitrary phrase combinations, and automatic plural inflation. Alcohol synonyms/spelling variants were conservatively curated; raw 324-lemma alcohol subtree was not imported wholesale. Generic bar terms and adjectives still need editorial topic-fit review; brands and homographs require final review. Existing word store matches are recorded but are not lexical proof.

No 576-word Standard or 1200-word Signature candidate is claimed. See validation.json for measured shortfalls and per-niche block upper bound; no puzzle groups are approved.
